## Recoverable knowledge

Internal is an ownership/definition boundary, not a confidentiality mechanism; final composition may read internal metadata. A unique type's merge behavior differs from readOnly's raw definition-count constraint. Direct internal unique values were migrated to base types plus readOnly, with overridden defaults removed and producer/consumer responsibilities reviewed. Nested/element uniqueness is not mechanically replaced.

User UID is a final-composition input and loses internal tagging, while a host's produced numeric registry remains internal. Provider fallbacks belong in the consumer view, not extra definitions on readOnly options. Disabled providers are not implicitly enabled by absence. Historical operator/style changes distinguish Nix expressions from shell strings and do not by themselves prove runtime equivalence.
